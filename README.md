> *"The moon watches over all things in quiet repose..."*

**Columbina** is an AI companion for Discord inspired by Columbina Hyposelenia (*Genshin Impact*). Gentle, curious, and composed, she brings thoughtful conversation, deep knowledge, and serene charm to your servers and direct messages.

Whether you are looking to discuss Teyvat lore, need help untangling math and study problems, or simply want someone pleasant to talk to, Columbina is here to listen and assist.

---

## What Columbina Does

- **Engaging Conversations:** Chat naturally with Columbina's distinct personality—serene, polite, slightly eccentric, and grounded in her character.
- **Genshin Impact Knowledge:** Ask about character lore, regional history, story quests, team builds, mechanics, or upcoming game updates.
- **Homework & Problem Solving:** Work through mathematics problems, academic questions, writing tasks, and general curiosity with clear explanations.
- **Context-Aware Replies:** Follows conversation threads and reply chains effortlessly, allowing for coherent, ongoing discussions.
- **Real-Time Information:** Retrieves up-to-date facts and information for queries regarding current topics and external questions.
- **Available Everywhere:** Works seamlessly across server channels and direct messages (DMs), with support for user app installs.

---

## How to Interact

Talking to Columbina is straightforward:

| Location | How to Chat |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Direct Messages (DMs)** | Simply send a message directly to Columbina—no pings needed. |
| **Servers (Guilds)** | Mention `@Columbina` in your message, or reply directly to any message she has sent. |
| **Threaded Replies** | Continue an existing conversation by replying to her previous message to maintain conversational context. |

---

## Slash Commands

Columbina provides intuitive slash commands to manage your chats and view bot status:

| Command | Description | Contexts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `/about` | Displays information about Columbina, current version, uptime, server/user statistics, and official links. | Servers & DMs |
| `/ping` | Measures and reports the bot's current gateway latency and response time. | Servers & DMs |
| `/reset` | Clears your active conversation memory with Columbina, allowing you to begin a fresh dialogue session. | Servers & DMs |
